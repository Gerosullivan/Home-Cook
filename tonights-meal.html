Pan-fried Salmon with Crushed Minted Peas & Lemon Butter Potatoes

~30 mins total
Serves 5
Salmon · Spring
No plan tonight — nothing on the calendar or in the meal plan, so this is a Chef's Pick. Salmon is in season at the fishmonger, so pick up five fillets on the way home if you fancy it. Otherwise, swap in cod or hake — both work the same way.

Ingredients

  • 5 salmon fillets, skin on (~140g each)
  • 800g baby potatoes (or Maris Piper, chunked)
  • 400g frozen peas
  • Small bunch of fresh mint
  • 60g butter (plus extra for the pan)
  • 1 lemon (zest + juice)
  • 1 small clove of garlic
  • 2 tbsp crème fraîche or double cream (optional)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Flaky sea salt & black pepper

Prep Ahead

~15 min prep
Before you leave for pickup at 5:00 PM
Wash and halve the baby potatoes (or chunk the Maris Pipers into 3cm pieces). before
Pick the mint leaves off the stems and finely chop. Crush the garlic clove. Zest the lemon, then cut into wedges. before
Boil the potatoes in well-salted water for 15–18 mins until knife-tender. Drain, return to the warm pan, lid on. They'll keep grand for half an hour. before
Pat the salmon fillets dry with kitchen paper. Season the flesh side with salt and pepper. Pop back in the fridge until you're ready to fry. before
Tip the frozen peas into a bowl on the counter so they start thawing while you're at pickup. during
While you're at pickup
Potatoes drained and resting in the warm pan — ready to crush in butter
Salmon seasoned and chilled — just needs a hot pan
Peas thawing on the counter — one less thing to wait on
Food safety: Wash the board and knife after handling raw fish. If the kitchen is warm, keep the seasoned salmon in the fridge until you're back. Probe-thermometer target: salmon at 52–55°C in the thickest part for moist, just-cooked.

Method

1
Crush the potatoes 3–4 mins
Add 30g of butter, a squeeze of lemon and a pinch of salt to the pan of potatoes. Use a fork to half-crush them so they soak up the butter — rough, not mashed. Lid back on, low heat to keep warm.
2
Get the salmon pan ripping hot 2 mins
Put a non-stick or heavy frying pan on a high heat with the olive oil. You want it shimmering before the fish goes in — that's how you get crispy skin.
3
Fry the salmon, skin-side down 4–5 mins
Lay the salmon fillets in skin-side down. Press each one gently with a fish slice for the first 10 seconds so the skin stays flat. Don't move them. Cook 4–5 mins until the skin is crisp and the flesh has gone opaque about two-thirds of the way up the side.
4
Crush the peas 3 mins (in parallel)
While the salmon's frying, melt 30g butter in a second small pan. Add the crushed garlic for 30 seconds, then tip in the peas. Cook 2 mins until hot through. Off the heat, add the chopped mint, a squeeze of lemon, and the crème fraîche if using. Crush with a potato masher to a rough texture — not a purée.
5
Flip and finish the salmon 1–2 mins
Flip the salmon and cook another 1–2 mins on the flesh side — aim for 52–55°C internal with the thermometer. Off the heat, squeeze over a little more lemon.
6
Plate up
Spoon the minted peas onto each plate, lay a salmon fillet on top, skin-side up so it stays crisp. Crushed lemon-butter potatoes on the side. Scatter with the lemon zest and a few mint leaves.
Kid-friendly note: The little ones often prefer salmon flaked off the skin and into the buttery potatoes. For the 2- and 3-year-olds, mash a portion of peas straight into the spuds — takes the edge off the mint and they hoover it up. Skip the crème fraîche on their portion if you'd rather.
