🍜 Chicken Pad Thai

~30 mins total
Serves 5
Chicken · Thai · Week 3 Friday
🍜 Week 3 Friday: Homemade takeaway night — RecipeTin Eats’ Chicken Pad Thai, scaled up from 3 to 5 servings. All the prep fits comfortably before pickup so you’re just tossing a wok for ten minutes when you’re back.

Ingredients

  • Sauce
  • 2½ tbsp tamarind puree (not concentrate)
  • 5 tbsp brown sugar, packed
  • 3½ tbsp fish sauce
  • 2½ tbsp oyster sauce
  • Stir-fry
  • 210g dried rice stick (pad thai) noodles
  • 5 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 250g chicken breast or thigh, thinly sliced
  • 3 eggs, lightly whisked
  • 210g firm tofu, cut into 3cm batons
  • 125g beansprouts
  • 40g garlic chives, cut into 3cm pieces
  • To finish
  • 60g peanuts, finely chopped
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • Ground chilli or cayenne (on the side, optional)

Prep Ahead

~20 min prep
Before you leave for pickup at 5:00 PM — everything chopped, sauced and measured so the wok is 10 minutes flat when you’re home.
Whisk the sauce: 2½ tbsp tamarind, 5 tbsp brown sugar, 3½ tbsp fish sauce, 2½ tbsp oyster sauce in a small bowl until the sugar dissolves. before
Slice the onion finely and chop the garlic. Cube the tofu into 3cm batons and pat dry. Cut the garlic chives into 3cm lengths. before
Thinly slice the chicken (easier when fridge-cold). Season with a pinch of salt, cover and return to the fridge. before
Lightly whisk the 3 eggs in a small bowl. Rinse the beansprouts and leave draining in a colander. Finely chop the peanuts, cut the lime into wedges. before
Soak the rice noodles in a bowl of just-boiled water for 5 minutes, then drain and rinse well under cold water. Toss with ½ tsp oil so they don’t stick, cover. before
While you’re at pickup
Raw chicken in the fridge, sauce on the counter, noodles covered and oiled. The wok stays cold — this dish wants one hot, fast pass when you’re back.
🧼 Food safety: Wash your hands, board and knife right after slicing the raw chicken. The kitchen can be warm this time of year — keep the sliced chicken in the fridge while you’re on the school run, not on the counter.
👧 Kid-friendly trick: Before you add the sauce to the wok, scoop out a small portion of plain buttered noodles with a bit of chicken and egg for Child 3 (2 yo). Tamarind and fish sauce are punchy — the plain version is more her speed. Keep the chilli and extra peanuts strictly on the side for the grown-ups.

Method

1
Fire up the wok ~1 min
Get your biggest non-stick pan or wok screaming hot over high heat. Have every bowl lined up beside it — sauce, chicken, egg, noodles, chives, beansprouts. Once you start, you don’t stop.
2
Garlic, onion & chicken ~2 mins
Add 3 tbsp of the oil. Tip in the garlic and onion, toss 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the chicken and stir-fry 1½ minutes until it’s mostly cooked through — still a hint of pink is fine, it finishes in a moment.
3
Scramble the eggs ~1 min
Push everything to one side of the pan. Pour the last 2 tbsp oil and the whisked eggs into the cleared side. Let them set for 20 seconds, then scramble with the back of the spoon and fold the chicken back through.
4
Tofu, noodles & sauce ~2 mins
Add the tofu, most of the beansprouts (hold a handful back for the table) and the noodles. Pour in all of the sauce. Toss with tongs or two spatulas so every strand picks up colour.
🍜 Scoop-out moment: Right before you pour the sauce in, lift out a portion of plain noodles + chicken + egg into a small bowl — that’s Child 3’s dinner sorted. Then pour the sauce for the rest.
5
Toss until glossy ~1½ mins
Keep tossing for about a minute and a half — the noodles drink up the sauce and turn glossy reddish-brown. If it looks dry, a splash of water loosens things without watering down the flavour.
6
Chives & half the peanuts ~30 secs
Scatter in the garlic chives and half of the chopped peanuts. Toss once or twice — the chives should wilt but still have bite. Off the heat.
7
Plate & serve ~2 mins
Pile into bowls. Top with the remaining peanuts, the reserved fresh beansprouts and a lime wedge. A little pot of chilli on the side for Ger and Maura.
