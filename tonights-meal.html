🍯 Sticky Honey-Garlic Pork Mince Rice Bowls

~30 mins total
Serves 5
Pork · Asian fakeaway
👨‍🍳 Chef's Pick — Saturday fakeaway: Saturdays are normally takeaway night. This is a homemade alternative built entirely from what's on the shelf: pork mince from the freezer, sushi rice, frozen veg, and the well-stocked Asian sauce drawer. Sticky, sweet, mild — tailor-made for the kids. If you fancy ordering in instead, no harm done.
🧊 Defrost note: The pork mince needs defrosting. Pull 700g from the freezer first thing this morning into the fridge. If it’s late in the day, run it under cold water in its bag for 30–40 mins, or fall back to the takeaway.

Ingredients

  • Bowls
  • 700g pork mince
  • 350g sushi rice
  • 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp rapeseed/groundnut oil
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • Sticky honey-garlic sauce
  • 4 tbsp honey
  • 4 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp mirin
  • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp cornflour + 2 tbsp cold water (slurry)
  • Sesame veg side
  • 300g frozen broccoli florets
  • 150g frozen sliced carrots
  • 150g frozen peas
  • ½ tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp honey
  • To finish
  • 2 tbsp toasted sesame seeds
  • Pinch of dried chives (optional)

Prep Ahead

~12 min prep
Saturday is gentler than a school night — no 5:00 PM pickup pressure. But everything below can be done any time before dinner so the cook is a calm 25 minutes start to finish.
Pull 700g pork mince from the freezer to the fridge first thing — ideally a full day ahead, otherwise as early as possible. before
Whisk the sauce: 4 tbsp honey, 4 tbsp light soy, 1 tbsp dark soy, 2 tbsp mirin, 2 tbsp oyster sauce, 1 tsp rice vinegar. Cover. before
Finely chop 4 cloves of garlic. In a tiny bowl, mix 1 tsp cornflour with 2 tbsp cold water — this is your slurry, set aside. before
Rinse 350g sushi rice in cold water until it runs almost clear (3–4 changes). Drain well, leave in the rice pot ready to switch on. before
Toast 2 tbsp sesame seeds in a dry pan over medium heat, shaking 1–2 mins until golden. Tip into a small bowl — do this now so the pan’s free for the pork. before
Measure veg into one bowl: 300g broccoli, 150g carrots, 150g peas. Mix the veg dressing in another tiny bowl: ½ tbsp sesame oil + 1 tbsp light soy + 1 tsp honey. before
What sits well
Sauce, slurry, garlic, dressed veg can all live in the fridge for hours. Rinsed rice in the pot is fine on the counter for an hour or two before you switch it on.
🧼 Food safety: Wash hands, board and the bowl after handling raw pork mince. Defrosted mince should go straight into the fridge until cook time — don’t leave it on the counter.
👶 Kid-friendly trick: Before you stir the sauce into the mince, scoop out a small portion of plain browned mince + plain rice for Child 3 (2 yo) and Child 2 (3 yo) if needed. The sauce is sweet and mild but salty — the youngest two often prefer the plain version. Sesame seeds on top of theirs is fine.

Method

1
Rice on first ~12 mins active, then 10 mins rest
Tip the rinsed sushi rice into the pot with 525ml cold water (1.5× the rice volume) and a small pinch of salt. Lid on, bring to a boil, then drop to the lowest simmer for 12 minutes. Off the heat, lid still on, leave 10 minutes to steam. Don’t lift the lid in between.
2
Brown the pork ~6 mins
While the rice simmers: heat a wide heavy pan over medium-high. Add the rapeseed oil, then the pork mince. Break it up with a wooden spoon and let it sit for 2 minutes to take colour, then start stirring. Cook 4–5 minutes more until no pink remains and any liquid has steamed off. Drain off excess fat if it looks oily — you want about a tablespoon left.
3
Garlic + sesame oil ~30 secs
Drop the heat to medium. Push the mince to one side, add the sesame oil and chopped garlic to the cleared spot. Sizzle 30 seconds until fragrant but not browning, then stir through the mince.
🥄 Scoop-out moment: Right now, before any sauce hits the pan, lift out a portion of plain mince for the youngest if they’d prefer. Pair it with plain rice and a few peas.
4
Sauce in — reduce ~2 mins
Pour in the whisked sauce. Stir, bring to a bubble, simmer 1–2 minutes — you’ll see it thicken and start to glaze the meat.
5
Slurry — gloss it up ~1 min
Stir the cornflour slurry (it settles, so re-mix it). Pour it in while stirring — the sauce will instantly tighten into a glossy, sticky coat. Take off the heat once it clings to the mince.
6
Steam the veg ~5 mins, in parallel
While the sauce reduces (step 4), microwave-steam the broccoli, carrots and peas in a covered bowl with 2 tbsp water for 4–5 mins on full power until tender but bright. Drain. Toss with the veg dressing (sesame oil + soy + honey).
7
Build the bowls ~2 mins
Fluff the rice with a fork. Bowl up: a bed of rice, a generous spoon of sticky pork mince on top, the sesame veg tucked alongside. Shower with the toasted sesame seeds and a pinch of dried chives if you fancy. Serve right away.
