🌿 Pork & Lemon Polpette with Orzo, Peas & Brown Butter

~40 mins total
Serves 5
Pork · Italian-ish · Spring
👨‍🍳 Chef’s Pick: Nothing on the meal plan tonight, so this is an off-rotation original built around what’s in your kitchen — pork mince from the freezer, lemons in the fruit bowl, and the bag of frozen peas. Tender little polpette in a glossy lemon-butter sauce, tossed through orzo. Light, springy, Sunday-tea food. Not in the database — if you like it, give it a thumbs up at check-in and we’ll add it.

Ingredients

  • Polpette
  • 500g pork mince (defrosted)
  • ½ white onion, finely grated
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • Zest of 1 lemon
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 2 tbsp plain flour
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp salt, good twist of pepper
  • Sauce & Orzo
  • 350g orzo
  • 2 tbsp rapeseed oil
  • 60g butter (40g sauce + 20g orzo)
  • 2 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 400ml chicken stock
  • Juice of 1 lemon (~3 tbsp)
  • 200g frozen peas
  • 1 tsp dried parsley, to finish
  • To serve (optional)
  • 1 extra lemon, cut into wedges
  • 1 tbsp basil pesto, swirled in

Prep Ahead

~22 min prep
Before you leave for pickup at 5:00 PM — meatballs rolled and browned, sauce ready to finish in 8 minutes flat after pickup.
Defrost the pork mince if you haven’t already — cold-water bath in the sink for 30 mins works. Defrost 2 chicken stock cubes from the freezer (microwave 1 min) and dilute to ~400ml with hot water. before
Finely grate ½ a white onion, mince 2 cloves garlic for the polpette, and thinly slice 2 more cloves for the sauce. Zest 1 lemon and juice it (set juice aside in a small bowl). before
Mix the polpette: pork mince, grated onion, minced garlic, lemon zest, beaten egg, 2 tbsp flour, dried parsley and oregano, ½ tsp salt and pepper. Mix gently with your hands — don’t overwork it. Roll into about 20 walnut-sized balls (~25g each). before
Heat 2 tbsp rapeseed oil in a wide sauté pan or deep frying pan over medium-high. Brown the polpette in 2 batches, ~2 mins per side, just until they have a golden crust (still pink inside — they’ll finish in the sauce). Transfer to a plate, cover loosely with foil, leave on the hob (off the heat). before
Wash the meatball board, knife and your hands. Tip the frozen peas into a bowl and leave on the counter to take the chill off. Cut lemon wedges for the table. before
While you’re at pickup
Browned polpette resting on the plate, sliced garlic + stock + lemon juice on the counter, peas warming up. The pan keeps a head start of fond on the bottom — that’s the flavour base for the sauce.
🧼 Food safety: Wash your hands, the board and your knife thoroughly after handling the raw mince. If the kitchen is warm, slide the browned polpette into the fridge while you’re on the school run instead of leaving them on the hob.
👧 Kid-friendly trick: The polpette are mild — just lemon, herbs and garlic, no chilli. For Child 3 (2yo) you can plate a couple of meatballs straight from the pan with plain orzo and butter, before stirring in the lemon sauce, if she finds the lemon too tangy. The peas are a soft win.

Method

1
Boil water for the orzo ~5 mins to boil
First thing back in the door: a big pan of well-salted water on for the orzo. While it heats, do the next step.
2
Build the sauce ~2 mins
Lift the rested polpette onto a plate. Put the same pan on medium heat — there’ll be flavourful fond in the bottom. Add 40g of the butter and the sliced garlic. Sizzle gently 30 seconds — don’t let the garlic colour past pale gold.
3
Deglaze and simmer ~2 mins
Pour in the chicken stock and the lemon juice. Scrape the bottom of the pan with a wooden spoon to lift the brown bits. Bring to a gentle simmer.
4
Drop the orzo ~9 mins
Once the water’s at a rolling boil, tip in the 350g orzo. Stir for the first 30 seconds so it doesn’t clump. Cook 8–9 minutes for al dente — check the packet.
5
Finish the polpette in the sauce ~6 mins
Slide the browned polpette back into the simmering sauce. Cover and cook 5 minutes, turning halfway, until they’re cooked through (no pink at the centre, juices clear — the wireless thermometer should read 71°C if you’re probing).
🌡️ Probe time: If you’re using a wireless thermometer, push it into the largest meatball before you cover the pan. Pork polpette want 71°C internal — whip them out the moment they hit it so they don’t dry.
6
Add the peas ~2 mins
Tip the peas into the pan around the polpette. Let them simmer 1–2 minutes until vibrant green and just hot through. The sauce should be glossy and lightly thickened — if it’s thin, raise the heat for 30 seconds; if it’s tight, splash in a ladle of orzo cooking water.
7
Drain & toss the orzo ~1 min
Reserve a cup of the orzo cooking water, then drain. Tip the orzo back into the dry pot with the remaining 20g butter and a small splash of cooking water. Stir until glossy.
8
Plate & serve ~2 mins
Spoon the buttery orzo into shallow bowls. Top with 4 polpette per adult plate (3 for the kids), spoon the lemony sauce and peas over the top. Scatter the finishing dried parsley across. A wedge of lemon on the side, and a small swirl of basil pesto in the adult bowls if you fancy a herby lift.
