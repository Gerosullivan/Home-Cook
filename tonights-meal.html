Pork & Apple Meatballs with Buttery Mash & Onion Gravy

~20 min prep + 25 min cook
Serves 5
Pork · Irish Comfort
Sunday Chef's Pick: You've 1 kg of pork mince in the freezer — perfect for meatballs. The apples add a lovely sweetness the kids will go mad for, and the onion gravy is pure comfort food. Take the mince out of the freezer this morning to defrost in the fridge.

Ingredients

  • 800g pork mince (from freezer — defrost in fridge from morning)
  • 1 eating apple, peeled & grated
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 50g breadcrumbs (blitz a couple of slices)
  • 1 tsp dried sage
  • Salt & pepper
  • 2 tbsp rapeseed oil
  • 1 kg potatoes, peeled & quartered
  • 60g butter (40g for mash, 20g for gravy)
  • 80ml milk
  • 2 medium onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp plain flour
  • 350ml chicken stock
  • 2 sprigs fresh thyme (or ½ tsp dried)
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard

Prep Ahead

~20 min prep
Before you leave for pickup at 5:00 PM
Peel and quarter 1 kg potatoes. Place in a large pot of cold salted water, ready to boil before
Peel and grate the apple. Combine in a large bowl with 800g pork mince, 1 beaten egg, 50g breadcrumbs, 1 tsp dried sage, salt & pepper. Mix well and shape into ~20 meatballs (golf ball size) before
Place meatballs on a plate or tray, cover with cling film, into the fridge before
Thinly slice the 2 onions and mince the garlic. Set aside in a bowl before
Measure out 350ml chicken stock, 1 tbsp flour, and 1 tsp mustard. Set by the hob before
Chopping board reminder: Wash hands, board, and knife thoroughly after handling the raw pork mince before touching other ingredients.

Method

1
Defrost the mince morning job
Move the pork mince from the freezer to the fridge first thing. It'll be defrosted by the afternoon.
2
Mix & shape the meatballs 10 mins
In a large bowl, combine the pork mince, grated apple, beaten egg, breadcrumbs, dried sage, and a good pinch of salt & pepper. Mix with your hands until just combined — don't overwork it. Roll into ~20 meatballs, about golf ball size.
3
Start the potatoes after pickup
Put the pot of potatoes on a high heat. Once boiling, reduce to a simmer and cook for 18–20 minutes until fork-tender.
4
Brown the meatballs 8 mins
Heat 2 tbsp rapeseed oil in a large non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the meatballs in batches — don't crowd the pan. Brown on all sides, about 8 minutes total. They don't need to be cooked through yet. Transfer to a plate.
5
Make the onion gravy 12 mins
In the same pan, melt 20g butter. Add the sliced onions and cook for 6–7 minutes until soft and golden. Add the garlic and thyme, cook 1 minute. Sprinkle in the flour, stir for 30 seconds. Pour in the chicken stock and Dijon mustard, stir well, and bring to a simmer.
6
Simmer meatballs in gravy 10 mins
Nestle the browned meatballs into the gravy. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer for 10 minutes until the meatballs are cooked through and the gravy has thickened nicely. Stir gently once or twice.
7
Mash the potatoes 5 mins
Drain the potatoes and return to the pot. Add 40g butter and 80ml milk. Mash until smooth and creamy. Season with salt & pepper.
8
Serve 2 mins
Spoon a generous mound of mash onto each plate, top with meatballs and ladle over plenty of onion gravy. Pick out the thyme sprigs if you used fresh.
Kid-friendly note: The apple keeps the meatballs lovely and sweet — no spice at all. For the little ones, cut the meatballs in half so they cool faster. They'll hoover these up.
