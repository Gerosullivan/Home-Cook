🐟 Lemon-Thyme Roast Salmon with Crushed Baby Potatoes & Buttered Peas

~40 mins total
Serves 5
Salmon · Irish spring · Sunday Chef’s Pick
🐟 Sunday Chef’s Pick: Nothing on the calendar tonight and no fresh meat thawed — a spin past the fishmonger fixes that. Late spring is a sweet spot for Irish salmon, and the rest of this dinner comes straight out of the pantry and freezer. Light, bright, oven does most of the work.

Ingredients

  • From the fishmonger
  • 5 × ~150g salmon fillets, skin on
  • From the kitchen
  • 900g baby potatoes
  • 400g frozen peas
  • 80g butter, softened
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon (zest + juice, plus wedges to serve)
  • 4–5 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • A few handfuls fresh rocket (optional)
  • Sea salt & black pepper
🛒 Pickup list: Just the salmon. Everything else is already in stock — baby potatoes in the pantry, peas, butter and thyme to hand. Ask the fishmonger for centre-cut fillets of even thickness so they cook in sync.

Prep Ahead

~12 min prep
Sunday evening — no school-run constraints. These few jobs, done early, mean dinner is a 25-minute oven job with almost no fuss.
Make the herb butter: mash the softened butter with the zest of the lemon, the leaves stripped from 3 thyme sprigs, 1 grated clove of garlic, a good pinch of sea salt and a few grinds of black pepper. before
Halve any larger baby potatoes so they’re all roughly the same size. Tip into a pot, cover with cold water, salt generously, lid on. before
Pat the salmon fillets dry with kitchen paper, set on a plate skin-side down, lightly salt the flesh and leave in the fridge until you’re ready to cook. before
Cut the rest of the lemon into 5 wedges for the table. Pull the leaves from the remaining thyme sprigs for the peas. Slice the last 2 cloves of garlic thinly. before
While the oven preheats
Herb butter sits on the counter (softening makes it easier to dot). Salmon stays in the fridge until the very last moment — cold-from-the-fridge fish is fine to roast.
🌡️ Thermometer cue: Salmon, not chicken, so the target is different. Probe the thickest fillet at the 10-minute mark — pull from the oven at 50–52°C for moist, just-set salmon (it climbs another couple of degrees while it rests). If anyone prefers it fully opaque all the way through, take it to 60°C. Use one of the wireless probes so you’re not opening the oven door.
👧 Kid-friendly trick: Salmon is mild and buttery — usually a winner. Plate Child 3’s portion as flaked salmon, a few smashed potatoes with butter and a small pile of plain peas on the side. Keep the lemon wedge off her plate; the sharp citrus can be off-putting at 2.
🧼 Food safety: Wash the chopping board and knife well after handling the raw salmon — same drill as raw meat. Don’t let it sit out at room temp — back in the fridge between prep and roasting.

Method

1
Oven on, potatoes on the hob ~2 mins active
Heat the oven to 200°C fan (220°C conventional). Bring the pot of baby potatoes to a boil over high heat, then reduce to a steady simmer.
2
Tray up the salmon ~3 mins
Line a baking tray with parchment. Brush with 1 tbsp olive oil. Lay the salmon fillets skin-side down, well spaced. Dot two-thirds of the herb butter over the tops — save the rest for the peas and to finish.
3
Salmon into the oven ~12–14 mins, hands-off
Slide the tray onto the middle shelf. Set a timer for 10 minutes and slip a probe into the thickest fillet. Pull at 50–52°C for moist, just-set salmon (or 60°C if you prefer it fully cooked through). Tent loosely with foil and let it rest 3–4 minutes — it keeps cooking gently.
4
Drain & crush the potatoes ~3 mins
After about 18–20 minutes the potatoes should give easily to the tip of a knife. Drain well and tip back into the warm pot. Drizzle with the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, a knob of plain butter, a pinch of salt and a grind of pepper. Press each potato lightly with the back of a fork to half-crush it — don’t mash, you want craggy edges.
5
Buttered peas ~3 mins
In a small saucepan, melt about a third of the remaining herb butter with the sliced garlic over a low-medium heat for 30 seconds (don’t brown it). Tip in the frozen peas and a splash of water. Stir occasionally for 2–3 minutes until heated through and bright green. Off the heat, add a squeeze of lemon, the loose thyme leaves and a pinch of salt.
6
Plate & serve ~2 mins
Spoon the crushed potatoes onto each plate, peas alongside. Lay a salmon fillet on top, spoon over any buttery pan juices and dot the last of the herb butter so it melts into the fish. Scatter a few leaves of rocket if you want a bit of green and pepper. Lemon wedges to the table.
