🐟 Pan-Roasted Cod with Crushed Potatoes, Peas & Caper-Lemon Butter

~35 mins total
Serves 5
Cod · Coastal · Spring
👨‍🍳 Chef’s Pick: Nothing on the meal plan tonight, and yesterday was already a pork-mince supper. Spring cod is excellent at the fishmonger right now, so this one leans on a quick stop on the way home — five thick fillets, a bag of new potatoes, and a brown-butter sauce built from the pantry. Light, fast, springy. Not in the database — give it a thumbs up at check-in if you want it added to the rotation.
🛒 Pick up on the way home: 5 × thick cod fillets (~150g each, skin on if possible) and 700g baby/new potatoes. Everything else is in the kitchen. Tesco fish counter or Caviston’s on the way back from school will both work — cod is in good nick mid-spring.

Ingredients

  • Fish
  • 5 cod fillets (~150g each, ~750g total)
  • 1 tbsp rapeseed oil
  • ½ tsp sea salt, twist of pepper
  • 2 tbsp plain flour, for dusting
  • Crushed Potatoes
  • 700g baby/new potatoes
  • 25g butter
  • ½ tsp dried thyme
  • ½ tsp salt, pepper
  • Caper-Lemon Butter
  • 75g butter
  • 2 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp capers, drained & lightly chopped
  • Zest + juice of 1 lemon (~3 tbsp juice)
  • 1 tsp dried parsley
  • Peas
  • 250g frozen peas
  • 10g butter
  • ¼ tsp dried mint (optional)
  • To serve
  • 1 extra lemon, cut into wedges

Prep Ahead

~18 min prep
Before you leave for pickup at 5:00 PM — potatoes par-boiled, sauce ingredients ready in a bowl, cod patted & seasoned. After pickup, it’s an 18-minute finish.
Pop the baby potatoes in well-salted cold water in a wide pan. Bring to the boil, then simmer for ~12 minutes until just tender to a knife tip. Drain, return to the dry pan, lid on, leave on the counter to steam-dry. before
Thinly slice the 2 cloves garlic, drain & lightly chop the capers, zest 1 lemon into a small bowl and juice it into another. Cut the second lemon into 5 wedges for the table. Group all the sauce bits on one corner of the counter. before
Pat the cod fillets very dry with kitchen paper (this is the difference between a crisp top and a soggy one). Season both sides with salt & pepper, dust the top with a fine layer of plain flour, set on a plate skin-side down. before
Tip the frozen peas into a bowl on the counter to take the chill off — they’ll cook faster. Wash the board, knife and your hands. before
While you’re at pickup
Potatoes steam-drying in their pan, cod resting seasoned on its plate, sauce ingredients lined up, peas thawing on the counter. Nothing’s warming up to a danger zone — if the kitchen is over 22°C, slide the cod plate into the fridge for the half hour.
🧊 Food safety: Cod is delicate — if there’s any chance the kitchen will sit warm, put the seasoned fillets in the fridge while you’re on the school run rather than leaving them out. They’ll go straight from fridge to hot pan.
👧 Kid-friendly trick: Cod is mild and flaky — perfect for the younger two. Pull off a piece for each kid before you spoon over the caper butter (capers can be a hard sell for the 2-year-old). Plain crushed potatoes & buttered peas alongside, and a squeeze of lemon optional. The 8-year-old should love the lemony butter on top.

Method

1
Heat the oven and a pan ~5 mins
Set the oven to 200°C fan. Put a wide oven-safe frying pan on medium-high heat with the rapeseed oil. Get it hot — you want a shimmer, not smoke.
2
Crush the potatoes ~3 mins
In the par-boiled potato pan, add 25g butter, the dried thyme, and a pinch of salt & pepper. Use a potato masher or the back of a spoon to gently crush each one so it splits open but holds its shape. Lid on, off the heat, ready to go.
3
Sear the cod ~3 mins
Lay the cod fillets floured-side down in the hot pan. Don’t crowd — do them in 2 batches if your pan’s small. Press each fillet down gently for 5 seconds so they don’t curl. Sear undisturbed for 3 minutes until a proper golden crust forms.
4
Flip & finish in the oven ~5 mins
Flip the fillets carefully with a fish slice. Slide the whole pan into the oven for 4–5 minutes — cod is done when it flakes apart with light pressure (~52°C internal). Pull it out the moment it flakes; it’ll keep cooking on the way to the plate.
🌡️ Probe time: If you want to be precise, push the wireless thermometer into the thickest fillet before it goes in the oven. Cod wants 52°C internal — whip the pan out the moment it hits, since fish goes from perfect to dry in 30 seconds.
5
Cook the peas ~3 mins
While the cod is in the oven, drop the peas into a small pan with 50ml water and 10g butter. Lid on, medium heat, 2–3 minutes until vibrant green and just hot through. Drain off any excess water, finish with a pinch of salt and the dried mint if using.
6
Make the brown-butter sauce ~2 mins
Lift the cooked cod to a warm plate (don’t cover — you don’t want it to steam). Wipe the pan briefly, drop in 75g butter, melt over medium heat. Add the sliced garlic, swirl 30 seconds. As soon as the butter goes foamy and smells nutty, kill the heat and add the capers, lemon zest & juice and dried parsley. The juice will sputter — stand back. Swirl until glossy.
7
Plate & serve ~2 mins
Spoon a mound of crushed potatoes onto each plate. Top with a fillet of cod, then ladle the warm caper-lemon butter generously over the fish (and a little onto the spuds — it’s the best bit). A scattering of peas alongside, a lemon wedge on each plate, and you’re away.
