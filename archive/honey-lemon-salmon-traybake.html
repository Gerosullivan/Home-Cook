Honey & Lemon Salmon Traybake

~45 mins total
Serves 5
Salmon · Spring
Heads up: Saturday is normally takeaway night. This is a Chef's Pick suggestion in case you'd rather cook — one tray, one pot, in and out in about 45 minutes. Spring salmon from the fishmonger is in great shape right now. If you're going for the takeaway anyway, no harm done.

Ingredients

  • 5 salmon fillets (about 130g each)
  • 800g baby/new potatoes
  • 300g frozen peas
  • 2 lemons (1 zested & juiced, 1 in wedges)
  • 3 tbsp runny honey
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard (mild)
  • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 30g butter
  • Small bunch fresh dill (or parsley)
  • Salt & black pepper
  • Fresh rocket, to serve (optional)

Get Ahead

~15 min prep
Optional — knock these out anytime in the afternoon to make the cook itself painless
Scrub baby potatoes, halve any larger than a golf ball before
Whisk glaze: 3 tbsp honey, 2 tbsp Dijon, juice of 1 lemon, lemon zest, crushed garlic, 1 tbsp olive oil, salt & pepper before
Take salmon out of the fridge 20 mins before cooking — it cooks more evenly from room-temp before
Roughly chop dill, cut second lemon into wedges, set aside before
Wash rocket and shake dry, into a bowl ready for the table before
While the potatoes are roasting
Glaze the salmon and let it sit at room temp — 10–15 mins makes the flavour land deeper
Boil the kettle for the peas — saves you a few minutes later

Method

1
Preheat & par-boil potatoes 15 mins
Heat oven to 200°C fan / 220°C conventional. Bring a pot of salted water to the boil. Add the baby potatoes and simmer 10 minutes until just tender to a knife tip. Drain and shake the colander a few times — those rough edges crisp beautifully in the oven.
2
Crush & roast potatoes 20 mins
Tip potatoes onto a large baking tray. Drizzle with 1 tbsp olive oil, season with salt & pepper. Use the back of a fork to gently squash each one to about 1cm thick. Roast on the top shelf for 20 minutes while you prep the salmon.
3
Make the glaze 3 mins
In a small bowl whisk 3 tbsp honey, 2 tbsp Dijon, juice and zest of 1 lemon, crushed garlic, 1 tbsp olive oil, a generous pinch of salt and a few grinds of pepper.
Probe-it tip: Salmon is done at 52–55°C for moist medium, or 58°C for fully opaque. Stick a wireless thermometer into the thickest part of one fillet before you put the tray back in.
4
Add the salmon 12–15 mins
Pull the tray of potatoes out, push them to the edges. Pat salmon fillets dry, lay them skin-side down in the cleared centre. Spoon the glaze generously over each fillet. Return the tray to the oven for 12–15 minutes until salmon flakes easily and the glaze is sticky.
5
Buttered peas 5 mins
While the salmon is in: tip frozen peas into a small saucepan with a splash of boiling water. Cook 3 minutes, drain, return to pan with the butter, a pinch of salt and a small squeeze of lemon. Stir until glossy.
Kid-friendly note: The honey-mustard glaze is mild and sweet — the little ones love it. If anyone's wary, plate their salmon plain and serve a small dish of glaze on the side for dipping. Bare buttered peas and crushed potatoes are usually a hit too.
6
Plate & serve
Lift salmon and potatoes onto plates. Spoon over any tray juices. Top with chopped dill, add a lemon wedge and a small pile of rocket alongside. Buttered peas in a bowl in the middle of the table.
