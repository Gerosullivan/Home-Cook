Honey-Soy Glazed Salmon with Sesame Rice & Broccoli

~15 min prep + 15 min cook
Serves 5
Salmon · Asian-Inspired
Fishmonger stop: Pick up 5 salmon fillets (skin-on, ~150g each) on the way home. Spring salmon is gorgeous right now. Everything else is already in the kitchen — sushi rice, soy sauce, honey, sesame oil, and broccoli in the freezer.

Ingredients

  • 5 salmon fillets, skin-on (~150g each)
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 3 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely grated
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 300g sushi rice
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 bag frozen broccoli florets (~400g)
  • 1 tbsp rapeseed oil
  • 2 spring onions, sliced (optional garnish)
  • Pinch of salt

Prep Ahead

~15 min prep
Before you leave for pickup at 5:00 PM
Rinse 300g sushi rice in cold water until the water runs mostly clear (3–4 rinses). Add to a saucepan with 450ml cold water. Leave on the hob, ready to turn on before
Make the glaze: whisk together 3 tbsp honey, 3 tbsp light soy sauce, 1 tbsp sesame oil, grated garlic, and lime juice in a small bowl before
Pat salmon fillets dry with kitchen paper. Place skin-side down on a plate, spoon half the glaze over the top. Reserve the rest for later before
Take broccoli florets out of the freezer and place in a colander to start defrosting before
Toast sesame seeds in a dry pan for 1–2 minutes until lightly golden. Set aside before
While you're away
Salmon marinates in the fridge — put it in the fridge before you leave
Fridge reminder: Pop the salmon plate into the fridge while you're out — don't leave raw fish at room temperature.

Method

1
Cook the rice 15 mins
Turn on the hob under the rice saucepan. Bring to the boil, then immediately reduce to the lowest heat, cover with a tight lid, and cook for 12 minutes. Don't lift the lid. After 12 minutes, turn off the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes to steam.
2
Pan-fry the salmon 8 mins
Heat 1 tbsp rapeseed oil in a large non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat. Take the salmon out of the fridge and place skin-side down in the pan. Cook for 4 minutes until the skin is crispy. Flip and cook for 2 more minutes. Pour the reserved glaze over the fillets in the pan and let it bubble for 1–2 minutes until sticky and caramelised. Remove from heat.
3
Steam the broccoli 4 mins
While the salmon cooks, steam or microwave the broccoli florets for 3–4 minutes until tender but still bright green. Season with a pinch of salt and a tiny drizzle of sesame oil.
4
Season the rice 2 mins
Fluff the rice with a fork. Stir through 1 tbsp rice vinegar and most of the toasted sesame seeds, keeping a few back for garnish.
5
Serve 2 mins
Mound the sesame rice onto plates. Lay a glazed salmon fillet alongside with the broccoli. Drizzle any remaining pan glaze over the salmon. Scatter with remaining sesame seeds and sliced spring onion if you have it.
Kid-friendly note: The honey-soy glaze is sweet rather than spicy — kids love it. For the little ones, flake the salmon into smaller pieces and mix through the rice so it's easier to eat. Skip the spring onion garnish for them.
