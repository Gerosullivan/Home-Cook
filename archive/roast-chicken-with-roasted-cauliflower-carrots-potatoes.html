Roast Chicken with Roasted Cauliflower, Carrots & Potatoes

~1 hr 30 mins total
Serves 5
Chicken · Sunday Roast

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (~1.8kg)
  • 800g potatoes, peeled & quartered
  • 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 4 carrots, peeled & cut into chunks
  • 2 parsnips, peeled & cut into batons
  • 1 whole garlic bulb
  • 2 bell peppers, deseeded & chunked
  • 2 courgettes, sliced into thick rounds
  • 3 tbsp rapeseed or olive oil
  • 30g butter, softened
  • Salt & black pepper
  • A few sprigs of fresh thyme (or 1 tsp dried)

Method

1
Season & roast the chicken before pickup
Rub the chicken all over with softened butter, season well with salt & pepper. Place in a large roasting tin with the halved garlic bulb. Roast at 200°C fan for 1 hour 15 minutes total.
🌡️ Thermometer reminder: Insert the wireless probe into the thickest part of the thigh, avoiding bone. You're looking for 68°C internal temperature. The thermometer will alert you — no guessing.
2
Par-boil the potatoes 10 mins
After pickup, put the potatoes into a large pot of salted boiling water. Boil for 8–10 minutes until the edges are just starting to soften. Drain well and give the colander a good shake to roughen the edges — this is what makes them crispy.
3
Toss the root veg 5 mins
In a large bowl, toss the par-boiled potatoes, carrots, parsnips, and cauliflower with 2 tbsp oil, salt & pepper. Spread onto a large baking tray (or around the chicken if there's room).
4
Roast the veg 35–40 mins
Put the root veg tray into the oven alongside the chicken. After 20 minutes, add the bell peppers and courgettes tossed in the remaining 1 tbsp oil — these cook faster and will go mushy if added too early.
5
Rest the chicken 15 mins
Once the chicken hits 68°C, remove from the oven and transfer to a board. Cover loosely with foil and rest for 15 minutes. Don't skip this — the juices redistribute and it'll be much juicier. Leave the veg in the oven to finish crisping.
6
Quick gravy 5 mins
Pour off most of the fat from the roasting tin, leaving the juices and roasted garlic. Squeeze the soft garlic cloves out of their skins into the tin. Place on the hob over medium heat, add a splash of boiling water (or stock if you have it), and scrape up the sticky bits. Simmer for 2–3 minutes until slightly thickened. Season to taste.
7
Carve & serve
Carve the chicken and arrange on a platter with all the roasted veg. Pour the gravy into a jug. Serve family-style — everyone helps themselves.
Kid-friendly note: No heat or spice in this one. The roasted carrots and parsnips go lovely and sweet in the oven. If the younger ones are picky about mixed veg, serve the different veg in separate piles on their plates rather than all together.
Leftovers: Strip any remaining chicken off the bone tonight and pop it in the fridge. Great for sandwiches tomorrow or a quick chicken stir-fry later in the week. The carcass makes a brilliant stock if you're bothered.
