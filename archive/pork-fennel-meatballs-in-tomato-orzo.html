🍅 Pork & Fennel Meatballs in Tomato Orzo

~45 mins total
Serves 5
Pork · Tuscan-style · One pan
👨‍🍳 Chef’s Pick: Nothing on the meal plan tonight, and last night was cod. Time for something hearty — the freezer has 2 × 500g of pork mince and the pantry is thick with tinned tomatoes & orzo. This is a one-pan job: fennel-spiked little pork meatballs simmered in tomato orzo until silky and the pasta has soaked up all the sauce. No shopping run, no oven, no fuss. Not in the database — thumbs up at check-in if you want it added to the rotation.
❄️ Defrost first: Take 1 × 500g pork mince out of the freezer first thing this morning and put it in the fridge. If you’ve forgotten, sit it in a sealed bag in a bowl of cold tap water from ~3:00 PM — change the water every 30 mins. It’ll be ready by prep time.

Ingredients

  • Meatballs
  • 500g pork mince
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds, lightly crushed
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ small white onion, very finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely grated
  • 1 large egg, lightly beaten
  • 30g plain flour
  • ½ tsp fine sea salt, twist of pepper
  • 1 tbsp rapeseed oil, for browning
  • One-pan tomato orzo
  • 1 large white onion, finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • 1 × 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 300g orzo pasta
  • 800ml hot chicken stock
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp salt, twist of pepper
  • To finish
  • 100g frozen spinach (small handful)
  • 1 tsp red wine vinegar
  • 25g butter, off the heat
🥫 All from the kitchen: Every ingredient is already in stock — no shop run needed. Pork mince + tinned tomatoes + orzo + frozen stock cubes + frozen spinach + the spice rack do the heavy lifting.

Prep Ahead

~22 min prep
Before you leave for pickup at 5:00 PM — meatballs rolled and chilling, sauce ingredients lined up. After pickup it’s a 30-minute one-pan finish.
Boil the kettle. In a measuring jug, melt 2 frozen chicken-stock cubes with 800ml just-boiled water. Set aside on the counter to cool. before
Finely chop 1 large white onion + 3 cloves garlic for the sauce, and ½ small white onion + 2 cloves garlic (grated) for the meatballs. Keep the two piles separate. before
Lightly crush the fennel seeds in a mortar (or under a heavy pan) — just bruise them, don’t powder them. before
In a big bowl, combine the pork mince, finely chopped meatball onion, grated garlic, crushed fennel seeds, oregano, beaten egg, flour, salt & pepper. Mix gently with your hands — just until uniform, no longer (overmixing = tough meatballs). before
Roll into ~20 even meatballs (about 25g each, walnut size). Set on a tray lined with baking paper. Cover loosely and slide into the fridge. before
Open the tin of chopped tomatoes, measure out the tomato purée into a small dish, weigh out 300g orzo. Group everything by the hob. before
Wash the board, knife and your hands thoroughly — raw pork mince has been everywhere. before
While you’re at pickup
Meatballs firming up in the fridge (the chill helps them hold their shape when they hit the pan), stock cooling on the counter, sauce ingredients lined up. Nothing’s left out at risk.
🧊 Food safety: Raw pork mince — wash boards, knives, sink and your hands very well after rolling the meatballs. Don’t leave the meatball tray on the counter; the fridge is the right home for them while you’re on the school run.
👧 Kid-friendly: Mild and tomato-y — perfect for the younger two. The fennel is gentle, no chilli, no heat. Plate up the 2- and 3-year-olds’ portions before you stir in the spinach if greens are a hard sell tonight; stir it into the rest for the grown-ups and the 8-year-old.

Method

1
Brown the meatballs ~6–8 mins
Heat a wide deep sauté pan or shallow casserole over medium-high with the rapeseed oil. Take the meatball tray out of the fridge. Brown the meatballs in 2 batches — don’t crowd the pan. ~3 minutes per batch, turning once or twice, just until golden on the outside (they don’t need to be cooked through — they’ll finish in the sauce). Lift to a plate.
2
Soften the onion ~4 mins
Drop the heat to medium. Tip the chopped large onion into the same pan with a pinch of salt. Stir through the meaty bits stuck to the bottom — they’re flavour. Soften 4 minutes until translucent.
3
Garlic + tomato purée ~1 min
Add the chopped sauce garlic and the tomato purée. Stir for 1 minute until the purée darkens slightly and smells deep and savoury — this little step makes a big difference.
4
Build the sauce ~2 mins
Tip in the tinned tomatoes. Half-fill the empty tin with water, swirl, and add that too. Stir in the orzo, the stock, oregano, and salt & pepper. Bring to a gentle bubble.
5
Nestle in the meatballs ~12–14 mins
Tuck the browned meatballs back into the pan along with any juices from the plate. Lid on loosely (not fully sealed — you want a touch of evaporation). Simmer on medium-low for 12–14 minutes, stirring gently every few minutes so the orzo doesn’t catch on the bottom. It’s ready when the orzo is tender, the sauce is silky, and the meatballs are firm and cooked through.
🌡️ Probe time: If you want certainty on the meatballs, push the wireless thermometer into the largest one. Pork wants 71°C internal — it should be there comfortably after the simmer. Pull off the heat the moment they hit it.
6
Spinach + finish ~3 mins
Plate up the kids’ portions now if they’re anti-greens (see the kid note). Then stir the frozen spinach straight into the pan — the residual heat will thaw and wilt it in 1–2 minutes. Splash in the red wine vinegar to lift the tomato, then take off the heat and swirl in the butter for gloss. Taste — adjust salt.
7
Serve ~1 min
Spoon into shallow bowls — ~4 meatballs per adult, 2–3 for the kids, plenty of orzo. A grind of black pepper on top. If anyone’s asking for cheese, parmesan would land beautifully here — none in stock tonight, so add it to the next shopping list.
